# Let the supervisor record for the crew

@trace spec:research-records

## Claim

The farm labour tools I could find described in use do not depend on every worker operating an app.
The person with the phone and the motive does the recording.

## Evidence

- Good Fruit Grower (trade press): at FirstFruits Farms, Washington, each crew boss manages 48 or more
  workers, scanning a badge barcode at check-in, logging breaks, changing tasks and checking out; the
  pictured case is an H-2A worker. Gilbert Orchards scans employee badges and bins. Reported benefits:
  no end-of-week spreadsheets, payroll sync, and catching labour problems within a day rather than weeks.
- Counter-example: Sundquist Fruit chose FieldClock because it worked on existing employee phones, and
  another article describes most workers carrying the app. Worker phones can work too.
- Advice from growers: start small and prioritise goals; find an executive champion.
- Limits: these are grower and vendor voices with payroll as the driver. No worker views, no usage
  numbers, no failed rollouts. Survivorship bias is likely. Tasker's stated purpose is not payroll, so
  the motive that drives these apps may be missing. I could not confirm how they behave with no signal.
- Sambasivan et al. (peer-reviewed ethnography, two Bangalore slums, not farms) name proximate enabling,
  proximate translation and surrogate use: a skilled person acting for someone else is normal, not a
  failure. I saw the abstract only. The primary authority above applies to this part alone; the farm
  claim rests on trade press, hence the strength grade.
- General adoption studies I saw (Irish family farms; a UTAUT paper at AMCIS 2018) point to social
  influence on first use. They study farmers, not labourers, so I do not lean on them. Vendor articles
  on frontline apps were opinion and I excluded their numbers.

## How it applies

- As per research:field-adoption/supervisor-operated-capture-orchard-apps, the farmer must be able to
  mark a task started or done for any worker, so a worker without a working phone is still in the system.
- Teach in person: the supervisor sets up the worker and does the first task beside them. Do not
  rely on tutorial screens to onboard anyone.
- Plan for one phone serving a crew: quick person switch by face or name, with the crew's list visible.
- Start with one crew and one kind of task. Pick the first farm's champion before building more.
