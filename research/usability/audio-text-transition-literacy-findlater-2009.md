# Audio with text helps some users move toward reading, not all

@trace spec:research-records

## Claim

Audio paired with text can wean semiliterate users toward reading, but illiterate
users keep needing the audio.

## Evidence

**Seen only through a search summary of the abstract; the PDF could not be
opened, so strength is capped at expert-opinion.** Participant counts and effect
sizes are unverified, and the setting is not known to match a phone task list.

Literacy here is not known to mean reading a second language. Applying this to
migrants who may read at home but not locally is an inference.

## How it applies

- As per research:usability/audio-text-transition-literacy-findlater-2009, audio
  stays permanently available; never phase it out by default or by usage count.
- Show the word beside the audio so users who can use it will pick it up.
- Do not plan on all workers becoming readers of the UI.
