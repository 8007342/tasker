# Icon plus label beats icon alone for novices

@trace spec:research-records

## Claim

For novices, unlabelled icons were no advantage. Adding a word to the icon was
rated easiest and performed at least as well.

## Evidence

**Seen only through a search summary of the abstract; the paper could not be
opened, so strength is capped at expert-opinion.** Sample size and task are
unverified.

Nothing visible describes the participants as low-literate, and this is a
desktop-era application, so the label's value for someone who cannot read at all
is not established here. The further reason to keep the word in Tasker comes from
the language-learning record.

## How it applies

- As per research:usability/icons-with-labels-wiedenbeck-1999, keep the icon and
  the word together on actions; do not design a label-free screen.
- Keep the word and the icon in the same place every time, a design choice.
